# Community code of conduct

Narwhal 3D contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation respectful, constructive, and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and technically specific.
- Critique code and behavior, not people.
- Welcome questions and contributors of different experience levels.
- Respect privacy, licences, authorship, and confidential work.
- Disclose uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Keep issue and review discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, doxxing, publishing private material, deliberate licence violations, or sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions, and project-representing spaces.
