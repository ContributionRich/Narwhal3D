# Compatibility target and independence statement

Narwhal 3D is an independently developed, open-source reimplementation targeting the observable command workflow, NURBS modeling behavior, interface conventions, and `.3dm` interoperability associated with **Rhinoceros 3D**.

Official product reference: https://www.rhino3d.com/

Narwhal 3D does not include or depend on the original application, its source code, binaries, icons, UI resources, documentation corpus, or other proprietary assets. It is built on independently licensed open-source components, including Blender, Open CASCADE Technology, and `rhino3dm`.

Compatibility is a goal, not a claim of completeness or certification. Narwhal is currently a preview with substantial missing commands, options, panels, file-format behavior, rendering, and platform support. See [STATUS.md](STATUS.md), [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md), and [docs/gaps.md](docs/gaps.md).

Narwhal 3D is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Robert McNeel & Associates. Product and company names are used only to identify the compatibility target and file-format ecosystem.
